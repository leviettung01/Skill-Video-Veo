# Thumbnail demo từ project sẵn có

Ba ảnh này được tạo bằng built-in image_gen từ ảnh thật của project. Prompt tái sử dụng áp dụng được trong Tool với model tạo ảnh đã cấu hình. Các model có thể cho kết quả khác nhau.

## Creator xưởng · Editorial sạch

- Style ID: `factory_creator_editorial`
- Project nguồn: `1738086213622335480`
- Ảnh demo: `Template/Thumbnail_Templates/samples/factory_creator_editorial_demo.png`
- Chế độ chủ thể: `character`

### Cấu trúc prompt

```text
REFERENCE ROLES: exact product / actual character if requested / video environment
SUBJECT: {product_name}; verified facts: {product_info}
ART DIRECTION: selected style, camera, lighting, palette, materials and composition
TEXT: exact Vietnamese headline {hook_text}; optional verified supporting copy
CONTINUITY: preserve reference identity, product geometry and video context
OUTPUT: one complete 9:16 thumbnail; mobile readability; 8% safe margins
NEGATIVE: no false price/claims, invented gifts, extra fingers or watermark
```

### Prompt style tái sử dụng

```text
Clean industrial creator editorial, preserve the supplied real character identity, hair, glasses and outfit. Naturally present the exact referenced product close to the camera, with at most one authentic product-detail inset. Preserve the recognizable workshop or video environment, softened behind the hero. White and pale steel blue palette, restrained warm yellow accent, asymmetric magazine composition, large readable navy headline, fine graphic lines and generous whitespace. Realistic daylight, natural hands and accurate product scale. No fabricated technical specifications, certification or price badges.
```

### Prompt đã tạo demo

```text
Create ONE complete professional vertical 9:16 Vietnamese AI thumbnail, production-ready photography and typography integrated as a single designed image. Clean industrial creator editorial. Preserve the supplied adult woman's exact face, black glasses, black hair, navy suit and pale blue shirt. Preserve the exact stainless digital kitchen scale with rounded rectangular brushed steel platform, black LCD strip and two circular silver buttons. Keep the genuine bright assembly workshop context from reference, softened but recognizable. Medium portrait with friendly natural expression, woman offset slightly right naturally presenting scale close to camera, one large neatly integrated scale detail near lower left, no repetitive product grid. Crisp white and pale steel blue palette, restrained warm yellow design accent, airy asymmetric magazine layout. Large navy Vietnamese headline at upper left 'CÂN BẾP GỌN XINH', smaller subtitle 'Gọn một góc bếp'. Restrained fine graphic lines, elegant whitespace, realistic studio daylight, sharp product details, no price or specs, no offers or certification. Product must be central to hierarchy, not a generic portrait. Use references only for the stated roles, preserve actual identity and product shapes. Correct Vietnamese diacritics, polished readable mobile hierarchy, no watermark. Generate a new complete composition, not just text over the original reference.
```
