# Bản sửa mỹ thuật V2 — clip thử 8 giây

Mục tiêu: kiểm tra lại crop, ánh sáng, màu và động tác bằng một clip trước khi sản xuất lại cả demo. Đây là prompt đề xuất từ phân tích nguồn, chưa phải kết quả render mới.

## Storyboard

Create one vertical 9:16 photorealistic food-commercial storyboard, exactly six equal panels in 2 columns and 3 rows, chronological reading order. No text or labels. Each panel is a standalone tightly composed video frame, not a wide kitchen photograph.

VISUAL IDENTITY: The same matte warm-ivory cooking pot with a pale interior, same bright white home kitchen, soft window light from camera left. Neutral ivory highlights, gentle warm midtones, no overall yellow cast. Background limited to a thin softly blurred strip with pale wood utensils. Realistic slender adult hands, natural short nails, no jewelry. Clear glass prep bowls, one wooden spatula and wooden chopsticks. Food-commercial macro lens look, selective focus with the whole contact point readable. Detailed wet surfaces, restrained specular highlights, no plastic CGI appearance.

COMPOSITION: Crop through the pot sides; do not show both handles, the entire circular rim, a wide countertop or a large black stove. The ingredient and its immediate contact surface dominate each panel. Preserve the same pot and light even as framing changes. Depict the middle of each action, with a visible source, moving ingredient and destination.

PANEL 1: High oblique close-up into the empty ivory pot. A clear glass bowl tilted from the upper right pours a continuous thin golden stream of oil. The stream lands sharply in focus and spreads into a translucent reflective pool. Crop most of the hand and bowl edge.
PANEL 2: Same pot now with the established oil film. A tilted glass bowl releases thin crushed lemongrass diagonally toward the oil; several pieces suspended mid-fall, the first pieces touching the oil with tiny bubbles. No water, tomato or seafood yet.
PANEL 3: Thin purple-white shallot slices tumble from a clear glass bowl onto the established lemongrass. Distinct translucent layers, natural gravity, tiny oil highlights. Tight framing on the falling slices and landing point.
PANEL 4: A wooden spatula enters from the upper right and pushes the established shallots and lemongrass across the oil in one short arc. Glistening aromatics with readable texture, no burning, no broth. The spatula tip and contact region are the focus.
PANEL 5: Glossy ripe red tomato wedges fall from a tilted glass bowl into the established aromatics. Focus on the tomato flesh and first contact with the oil; do not cover the food with a large hand.
PANEL 6: Clear broth pours from a small glass jug onto the existing tomatoes and aromatics, making small outward ripples. Light steam, not a fog bank. This is still the pale broth stage; no red seasoning, corn, mushrooms or shrimp yet.

No collage within panels, duplicated hands, warped tools, arbitrary garnish, missing ingredients, camera-visible equipment, laptop, keyboard, UI, arrows, typography or watermark. Match the specified crop in every panel; do not trade close-up framing for showing the whole pot.

## Image-to-video

Create one 8-second vertical 9:16 realistic cooking commercial from the supplied six-panel storyboard. Use the panels only as a chronological shot map. Each shot fills the entire output frame. Never display the sheet, borders, split-screen or multiple panels, even on the first frame. Six clean hard cuts, no morph transitions, no zoom out, no camera orbit. Match each panel's tight crop, pot identity, neutral ivory highlights and soft window light. Food and the contact action dominate; retain a narrow blurred kitchen background only where the panel shows it.

0.0–1.3s: Begin already on the tilted glass oil bowl. A continuous golden thread falls into the ivory pot and spreads into a thin reflective pool. Camera locked, focus on the stream's landing point. Cut before the hand starts another task.
1.3–2.6s: Glass bowl tilts and lemongrass falls into the existing oil. Pieces land with natural gravity and settle; tiny localized sizzling bubbles. No broth.
2.6–3.9s: Shallot slices slide over a glass bowl lip, fall onto the same lemongrass and settle into the oil. Keep focus on the slices and their landing point.
3.9–5.2s: The wooden spatula makes one short deliberate sweep through the aromatics, pushing them rather than passing through them. Ingredients settle behind the spatula. No extra full stirring cycle.
5.2–6.5s: Red tomato wedges slide out of a tilted glass bowl and land on the existing aromatics. Small realistic bounce, restrained oil movement. No seafood or garnish.
6.5–8.0s: A steady clear broth stream enters the same pot, raising the liquid level and pushing the existing tomatoes gently apart. Outward ripples and a small amount of steam. End on the settled broth surface; no sudden finished dish.

Synchronized close cooking sounds only: pouring, delicate sizzling, ingredient contact and wood touching the pot. No speech, music, subtitles or logos. Correct hand anatomy, physically connected utensils, consistent ingredient states and quantities. Do not turn the pot into another vessel or show the empty pot again after ingredients have been added.

## Các clip tiếp theo cần chỉnh theo cùng chuẩn

Nước lẩu: cốt đỏ hòa vào nước → khuấy tạo vân dầu đỏ cam → bọt sôi rõ → nấm rơi từ bát kính → ngô vàng rơi → đảo nhẹ. Mỗi shot ghi crop và điểm tiếp xúc riêng.

Hoàn thiện: thả tôm sống → cắt sang thời điểm tôm chín → thêm rau → nhấn rau nhẹ → cận thành phẩm trong nồi → đũa nâng tôm và nấm lên gần máy. Nếu muốn thêm mực/bò như bản tham khảo, cập nhật công thức và trạng thái từ đầu; không tự thêm vào cuối clip. Tránh múc sang bát vì làm mất bố cục hero đang cần học từ mẫu.
